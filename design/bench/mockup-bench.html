Goal

Wire two knife switches in a row so the lamp lights only when both are closed.

  • Lamp lights with A and B closed
  • Dark with only A
  • Dark with only B

Both blades are down, so current runs from the cell, through A, through B, and into the lamp. Open either one and the circuit is broken. That is AND.
